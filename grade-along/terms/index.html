Grade Along Teacher Plan Terms

Last updated: November 23, 2026

These terms cover Grade Along teacher plans, which one teacher buys with their own card. Grade Along is provided by Arrival House Media LLC ("Arrival House," "we," "us") of Ruskin, Florida, under the STORIGEN name. By buying or using a teacher plan, you agree to these terms.

1. Who a teacher plan is for

A teacher plan is for one teacher, for their own classes. It does not cover a school, a department or other teachers, and it may not be shared. Schools buy school plans from us directly.

3. Automatic renewal

  • Your plan renews automatically at the end of each period (each month or each year), and your card is charged the plan's price each period until you cancel.
  • Renewal reminders: before your plan renews, we send you a reminder email (through Stripe) with the renewal date and a link to manage or cancel your plan. We send this reminder before every renewal of a yearly plan, and before monthly renewals too.
  • Price changes: if we change the price of your plan, we will tell you by email at least 30 days before the new price applies. The new price starts at your first renewal after that notice. You can cancel before then.

4. Cancelling

  • You can cancel anytime in Manage billing on the teacher plans page.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal.
  • You keep access, and any papers left in the period you already paid for, until the end of that period.
  • Your account data is deleted 30 days after your plan ends.

5. Papers

  • One paper is used only when Grade Along returns a usable result: a usable writing draft, or a usable set of read test answers.
  • These do not use a paper: service errors, results that can't be used, failed or invalid uploads, cover-sheet-only reads, rubric reads and page splits.
  • Papers are added at the start of each paid period.
  • Unused papers expire at the end of that month or year. They do not carry over.
  • If a renewal payment fails, Stripe retries it. No new papers are added until the payment goes through. Papers you already paid for stay usable until the end of their period.

6. Refunds

  • If you have not graded anything in a period, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of that purchase or renewal. Email info@arrivalhousemedia.com.
  • Once any paper from a period has been used, that period is not refundable.
  • Using "Flag bad result" gives a paper credit back, not money.
  • If a payment is disputed or charged back, the account is paused until the dispute is resolved.

7. The teacher decides

Grade Along drafts scores and comments to help you grade. It does not replace your judgment.

  • Drafts have no effect until you review and approve them.
  • AI output can be wrong. If a draft looks wrong, your judgment controls.
  • Final grades and any high-stakes decisions remain your responsibility, and your school's.

8. Student work and acceptable use

  • Only upload student work you are allowed to share for grading. Follow your school's policies and student privacy law.
  • Student work stays yours (and your students'). Uploading it gives us only the permission needed to process it and return drafts to you.
  • We do not use student work to train AI models.
  • Do not upload real student work to the public demo at gradealong.com/demo. The demo is for sample work only.
  • Do not try to access anyone else's account, and do not resell or share Grade Along or your plan.

9. Languages

Grade Along works in English and Spanish. Translated feedback is produced automatically and may contain errors. Review feedback before it reaches a student.
