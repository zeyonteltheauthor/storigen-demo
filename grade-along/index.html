Prompt: Write a story about a small choice that changes what happens next.

The Last Bus Ticket

Maya wanted to get to the library before it closed. In her pocket was the last bus ticket.

At the stop, a boy held a broken umbrella. His paper bridge was getting wet. Maya looked down the road. Her bus was coming.

She could get on, or she could help him save the bridge. She put her ticket away and held the umbrella over his project.

The boy took the bridge into the library. Maya followed him. They was both late, but the librarian held the door for them.
