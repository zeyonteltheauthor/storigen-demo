Grade Along is for the STORIGEN team and invited schools.

or

The passcode is kept in this tab only. A school sign-in is kept on this device until you press Lock, or until the session ends (30 days). In team (passcode) mode, papers, names and scores are never stored on the site. When you sign in with a school account, first names and approved scores are stored for your school's live reports. Photos, PDFs and story text stay on this device and are not stored on the site. Drafts stay in this browser on this device only, and each signed-in account has its own.

A privacy policy, terms of service, and a school data agreement are needed before schools outside Arrival House use Grade Along.
